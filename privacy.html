AT&T Office Hub Privacy Notice

Draft notice: This page is a starter template and must be reviewed and updated by the bot operator before the application is made available to users.

The intended purpose of AT&T Office Hub is to support internal office communication, sales-production tracking, and non-monetary team competitions.

Information the bot may process

Depending on features enabled, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server usernames, role information, sales-submission details, production metrics, approval status, and competition records.

Customer information

Users must not submit customer names, phone numbers, account numbers, service addresses, or other customer-identifying information.

Use and access

Information should be used only for the office functions described above and access should be limited to authorized administrators and managers.

Retention and contact

The operator must define how long records are retained, how deletion requests are handled, and provide a working contact method before publishing this notice.
